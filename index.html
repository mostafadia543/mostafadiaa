The artist behind the frames

Small setup.
Big visual worlds.

I'm Mostafa Diaa, a 22-year-old Egyptian independent director, animator and multimedia designer. My signature approach combines the immediacy of rigged 2D characters with the depth, lighting and atmosphere of 3D environments.

I work as a one-person studio, taking projects from concept and storyboarding through character design, custom rigs, animation, world-building, compositing and sound. Working within real technical limits has made optimization part of my art direction — not a compromise, but a way to make a distinctive visual language possible.

“Stop managing four freelancers for one video. Let’s build a world instead.”
Creative pipeline
  • Concept & direction
    Story structure, visual language and storyboarding.
  • 2D / 3D characters
    Custom rigging, Smart Bones, multi-angle setups and expressive animation.
  • World-building & lighting
    Stylized 3D environments, custom shaders and cinematic rendering.
  • Compositing, VFX & sound
    2D/3D integration, atmospheric effects, edit and sound design.
